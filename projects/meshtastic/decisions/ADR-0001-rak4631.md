# ADR-0001 — Adopt the RAK4631 for AEG-002
**Status:** Accepted — *platform only*. Procurement and enclosure design **not yet approved**.  
**Date:** 2026-10-26  
**Owner decision:** User selected RAK4631.

## Constraints
Rugged field device; budget band USD $250+; required GNSS and display; weather-resistant enclosure; measured 48h operation.

## Candidates
| Platform | Strength | Limitation for this project |
|---|---|---|
| **RAK4631 WisBlock (selected)** | Meshtastic-supported nRF52840/SX1262, modular GNSS/OLED and base options, BLE, compelling power-efficiency | Custom housing, display soldering and power/ingress tests required |
| LILYGO T-Echo | Integrated e-ink/GPS, easy to carry | Less modular/custom-engineering emphasis; weather sealing still needs evidence |
| WisMesh TAP | Integrated GNSS/display and factory housing | Less custom mechanical engineering and unknown real runtime under target workload |
| ESP32-heavy touchscreen builds | Rich interface | Higher power risk under a 48h target; would need additional validation |

## Decision and rationale
Use **RAK4631 on RAK19007** with **RAK12501 GNSS**, **RAK1921 OLED**, US915 antenna hardware and custom sealed enclosure as the starting architecture. The choice favors available official documentation, low-power-oriented MCU, and ability to customize and record hardware details. A 48h runtime is an acceptance target, *not* an established vendor guarantee.

## Purchase dependencies
Exact US915 Client-Tracker bundle contents; suitable Arduino bootloader; matched RF connections; battery polarity and vendor limits; verified enclosure dimensions and waterproof pass-through components. No sale or supplier selection authorized by this ADR.

## Revisit
If power tests fail 48h target, if GNSS/display sleep is insufficient, or if enclosure cannot be made practically weather-resistant, change peripherals/housing and record new ADR rather than erasing this decision.
